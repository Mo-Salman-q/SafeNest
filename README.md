# SafeNest: Empowering Teenage Girls in Healthcare and Safety

SafeNest is a web-based platform designed to provide teenage girls with access to essential healthcare, safety resources, emotional support, and personal growth tools. It offers AI-powered features that enable users to seek guidance, manage crises, track emotions, and engage with a supportive community.

## 🛡️ Features

- **Guidance AI Assistant**  
  A conversational AI bot that allows users to safely ask questions or share concerns about harassment or personal issues and get supportive, non-judgmental guidance.

- **Crisis Control AI**  
  Step-by-step AI-driven instructions to help users act swiftly and effectively during emergencies or threatening situations.

- **Emotional Tracker**  
  A mental wellness feature where users answer a few questions to assess their emotional state. Based on results, it suggests helpful articles and resources.

- **Growth & Maturity Guide**  
  Personalized suggestions and task recommendations that help users grow emotionally, mentally, and socially.

- **Community Articles**  
  Users can read articles shared by other women, covering experiences, tips, and motivational content—building a supportive peer network.

- **Emergency Button**  
  Instantly sends the user’s location to trusted contacts and nearby authorities in case of emergencies.

## 🧠 Inspiration

The idea for SafeNest came from the need to create a digital safe space for teenage girls—many of whom hesitate to discuss sensitive issues. Inspired by real-world problems of gender inequality, safety concerns, and lack of accessible healthcare information, SafeNest provides an AI-driven platform to address these challenges.
